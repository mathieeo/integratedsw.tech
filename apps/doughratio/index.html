What DoughRatio does.

DoughRatio is a baker's percentage calculator that does the math the instant you type. Dial in hydration, salt, and starter, scale any recipe up or down by weight or number of loaves, and follow a timeline from mix to bake. Built for bread bakers who think in ratios.
