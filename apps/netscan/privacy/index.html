NetScan Pro privacy policy.

Short version: NetScan Pro collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, and no third-party trackers. Everything happens on your device.

What NetScan Pro stores on your device

Your content and settings are stored on your device (and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud account through Apple). We never receive this information and operate no servers that could collect it.

What we don't do

  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, or photos.
  • We do not run analytics or third-party trackers.
  • We do not sell personal data.

Permissions

If the app asks for a system permission (camera, local network, Face ID, photos…), it is used only to power the feature in front of you, processed on-device, and never transmitted to us.

Questions? Email matt@integratedsw.tech.
